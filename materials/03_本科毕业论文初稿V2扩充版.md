# 新式茶饮企业盈利模式研究——以蜜雪冰城为例（初稿V2扩充版）

## 摘要

近年来，新式茶饮行业在消费升级、连锁扩张、数字化运营和社交媒体传播的共同推动下快速发展，成为餐饮消费市场中竞争最充分、品牌更迭较快的细分领域之一。伴随行业进入相对成熟阶段，茶饮企业之间的竞争已经不再局限于产品口味和门店装修，而是逐渐转向价格定位、供应链效率、加盟管理、品牌传播和组织运营能力的综合竞争。蜜雪冰城作为大众价格带新式茶饮品牌的代表，凭借低价产品、高密度门店布局、加盟扩张模式、供应链体系建设和“雪王”品牌IP传播，在行业中形成了具有鲜明特征的盈利模式。

本文以蜜雪冰城为案例，基于商业模式理论、竞争优势理论、连锁加盟理论和供应链管理理论，运用文献研究法、案例分析法、财务资料分析法和归纳总结法，对蜜雪冰城盈利模式的构成、运行机制、优势、问题及优化路径进行系统分析。研究发现，蜜雪冰城的盈利逻辑并不是简单依靠终端门店零售利润，也不是主要依靠加盟费，而是通过低价产品吸引消费者，以加盟模式扩大门店网络，再依托供应链体系向加盟商持续供应原料、包装、设备和相关服务实现收入。该模式可以概括为“低价引流—加盟扩张—供应链供给—规模增效”的循环机制。

根据蜜雪集团2024年度业绩公告，集团2024年实现收入248.28874亿元、年度利润44.54203亿元，销售商品和设备收入为242.088亿元，加盟及相关服务收入为6.201亿元，加盟及相关服务收入仅占总收入的2.5%。截至2024年12月31日，集团门店总数为46,479家，其中加盟门店46,462家，加盟商数量20,976名。这些数据表明，蜜雪冰城总部层面的核心收入来源主要来自供应链供给，而非传统意义上的加盟费。本文认为，蜜雪冰城盈利模式的优势主要表现为价格定位清晰、门店规模庞大、供应链效率较高、加盟体系较成熟和品牌传播能力较强；但同时也面临低价竞争压力、加盟商管理难度、食品安全风险、产品同质化、品牌升级困难和海外扩张不确定性等问题。基于此，本文提出加强加盟商分级管理、提升供应链数字化水平、优化产品结构、强化食品安全管控、推进品牌升级和稳步拓展海外市场等建议，以期为新式茶饮企业优化盈利模式和实现可持续发展提供参考。

**关键词：** 新式茶饮；蜜雪冰城；盈利模式；加盟模式；供应链管理

## Abstract

In recent years, China's freshly made tea beverage industry has developed rapidly under the combined influence of consumption upgrading, chain expansion, digital operation and social media communication. As the industry becomes more mature, competition among tea beverage brands has gradually shifted from product taste and store decoration to comprehensive competition in price positioning, supply chain efficiency, franchise management, brand communication and organizational operation. Mixue Bingcheng, as a representative brand in the mass-market price segment, has formed a distinctive profit model based on low-price products, dense store networks, franchise expansion, supply chain integration and the Snow King brand IP.

Taking Mixue Bingcheng as a case, this paper applies business model theory, competitive advantage theory, franchise theory and supply chain management theory, and uses literature research, case analysis, financial data analysis and inductive summary methods to systematically analyze the composition, operating mechanism, advantages, problems and optimization paths of its profit model. The study finds that Mixue Bingcheng's profit logic does not simply rely on terminal retail profits or franchise fees. Instead, it attracts consumers through low-price products, expands its store network through franchising, and generates revenue by continuously supplying raw materials, packaging materials, equipment and related services to franchisees through its supply chain system. This mechanism can be summarized as “low-price traffic attraction - franchise expansion - supply chain supply - scale efficiency”.

According to Mixue Group's 2024 annual results announcement, the Group recorded revenue of RMB24.828874 billion and profit for the year of RMB4.454203 billion in 2024. Revenue from sales of goods and equipment reached RMB24.2088 billion, while revenue from franchise and related services reached RMB620.1 million, accounting for only 2.5% of total revenue. As of December 31, 2024, the Group had 46,479 stores, including 46,462 franchised stores and 20,976 franchisees. These data indicate that the core revenue source at the headquarters level mainly comes from supply chain supply rather than traditional franchise fees. This paper argues that Mixue Bingcheng's profit model has advantages such as clear price positioning, large store scale, high supply chain efficiency, relatively mature franchise system and strong brand communication capability. However, it also faces problems such as low-price competition, franchisee management pressure, food safety risks, product homogenization, brand upgrading difficulties and uncertainty in overseas expansion. Based on these findings, this paper proposes suggestions such as strengthening franchisee hierarchical management, improving supply chain digitalization, optimizing product structure, enhancing food safety control, promoting brand upgrading and prudently expanding overseas markets.

**Key words:** freshly made tea beverage; Mixue Bingcheng; profit model; franchise model; supply chain management
